SentinelAV 0.17.0 — generalized detection, explanations and verified removal

Every scanned file now gets a score, verdict, confidence and plain-language reasons
from format-specific indicators, generic YARA rules and local models. Unfamiliar
threats can now become actionable detections.

Added recursive in-memory archive reading (ZIP including AES, TAR, GZIP, BZIP2, XZ,
7z, and RAR with an unrar tool), Quick and Full scan targets, default real-time
protection, high-confidence automatic quarantine, a streaming encrypted vault,
one-step removal, built-in network heuristics and a command-line scan.

Fixed scans ending after five minutes, malformed and oversized files escaping analysis,
one damaged archive member hiding the rest, and quarantine being refused during
monitoring. Removed two hashes taken from the evaluation corpus.

Measured on unseen theZoo families: 68.0% of archives flagged by the full product and
64.0% without hashes or family rules. 0 of 3,201 legitimate files flagged. Files are checked after they are
written, so this release cannot guarantee blocking before execution.

SentinelAV 0.15.0 — installed learned malware classifiers
